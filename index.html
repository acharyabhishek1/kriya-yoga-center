About the center

A sanctuary in the city

Kriya Yoga Center weaves traditional yoga teachings with contemporary healing, offering a grounded space to breathe, feel, and return to what is whole within.

In the heart of Ho Chi Minh City, the studio holds quiet, candlelit rooms where nervous systems can exhale, bodies can soften, and minds are invited into stillness beyond daily noise.

Sessions are crafted for trauma and emotional healing, blending somatic awareness, breath-led practice, and classical yogic techniques that honor each student’s pace.

Whether you are new to yoga or deepening a long-term practice, the center offers a premium yet intimate experience rooted in lineage, humility, and everyday integration rather than performance.

Our approach

Rooted, gentle, contemporary

Each class is a meeting point of lineage and lived life in Saigon, honoring ancient methods while speaking gently to modern nervous systems and schedules.

Traditional Lineage
Held by timeless teachings

The center follows classical Kriya and Hatha frameworks, integrating mantra, mudra, and breath in a way that respects the roots of yoga rather than diluting them.

Students are encouraged to build steady, devotional practice rhythms instead of chasing novelty, allowing depth to unfold over time.

Trauma & Emotional Healing
Spaces where feelings can land

Classes are consent-based, inviting choice, pacing, and interoception so the body can release, rather than re-create, overwhelm.

Gentle somatic language and restorative shapes help soften armor, making room for grief, tenderness, and joy without pressure.

Modern Lifestyle Integration
Yoga that lives with you

Schedules are designed around work and family rhythms in Ho Chi Minh City, with early mornings, mid-day resets, and quiet evening practices.

Students leave with simple tools—breathing, micro-movements, short meditations—that can be carried into commutes, offices, and homes.
